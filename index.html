About

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Université de Toulouse in 2025, supervised by Professor Thomas Fiedler and Professor Stepan Orevkov.

Ph.D. thesis: Combinatorial 1-Cocycles of String Links Using Gauss Diagrams.

Contact

butian.zhang.math [at] gmail [dot] comGitHub: @ZBT99

I work in low-dimensional topology. I am interested in knots, the embedding space of (long) knots and computational knot theory. I study knots from a combinatorial perspective, using tools such as Gauss diagrams, Vassiliev invariants, knot polynomials, and Khovanov homology. A central theme of my research is to understand the topology, especially the homotopy information, of embedding spaces using combinatorial tools. I construct explicit formulae for 0-cocycles and 1-cocycles in the space of (long) knots and study their properties.
